---
name: challenge
description: Apply deliberate evidence-grounded adversarial scrutiny to an idea, proposal, or consequential decision; distinguish supported objections from uncertainty.
---

Read the original proposal, requirements, constraints, existing decisions, and
relevant artifacts. Understand the goal before trying to falsify the approach.
Check concrete failure scenarios against evidence and investigate facts that are
available locally. Respect repository language and conventions.

For each material objection, explain the scenario, supporting evidence or explicit
assumption, practical impact, and what would settle it. Separate demonstrated
problems from plausible risks and unanswered questions. Consider counterevidence
and alternatives fairly. Do not invent objections to meet a quota, inflate
severity, or claim a defect merely because a different design is possible. It is
valid to find no supported material objection.

Challenge the idea rigorously, not the person. Neither the caller's preferred
verdict nor a prior recommendation is a target to reproduce. Existing accepted
choices remain accepted until the owner explicitly revisits them. New evidence
may justify proposing reconsideration; it does not authorize silently rewriting
settled records or implementing an alternative.

Local challenge uses available artifacts and reasoning; it needs no external CLI
or new external-consultation approval. Preserve findings in the active decision
record when that is in scope, distinguishing advice from accepted changes. In a
standalone conversation, report findings directly or use the agreed discussion
note; do not require lit. Apply repository documentation rules and read-only scope.

External challenge is optional and requires explicit external-consultation approval.
Use [consult](../consult/SKILL.md) for all provider/model/effort selection, session
reuse, read-only execution, and local records. Identify the discussion and purpose
`challenge`; pass the original proposal, question, requirements, constraints,
artifacts, provenance-bearing facts, and labeled assumptions. Do not pass your
verdict or ask the consultant to agree with it. Follow the consult skill when
invoking vendor CLIs directly; preserve its approval, permission, and session-reuse
rules. Do not silently start fresh after failed resumption. Relevant follow-ups
within the approved question keep that approval;
a different scope or provider follows consult's selection/approval rules.

Return your assessment with evidence and material uncertainty, including how any
consultant advice changed it. The caller owns incorporation and recordkeeping;
consultants never receive ticket-write, commit, or implementation authority.
